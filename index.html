Measures the round-trip time of WhatsApp delivery receipts in your own "Message yourself" chat. A companion client reacts to one pinned anchor message, the phone acknowledges it, and the time between the two is the RTT. The recipient is always the account itself, so it's a measurement tool for devices you own.

Noema

Reinforcement learning where the agent never sees the state. The environment encrypts each state before the agent gets it, so the agent isn't looking at a grid, a pixel, or a coordinate. It's looking at ciphertext, and if you printed one of its observations you'd see noise.

A unified handheld built around the ESP32-S3 that brings RF, RFID/NFC, and IR into one interface: capture, analyze, store, and transmit signals without juggling three separate tools.

GantryMate‑200

A 3-axis gantry that screws and unscrews about 280 miniature 1 mm screws on a two-layer LED panel: 200 on the top layer, 80 below. ESP32, DRV8825 drivers and NEMA 17 motors, plus a custom toolhead. The target is sub-millimeter positioning and under 5 seconds per screw. A team build with SRA-VJTI.
